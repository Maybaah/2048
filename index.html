Arcade Leaderboard

Slide every tile with the arrow keys, WASD or a swipe. Two tiles of the same number merge into their sum and add it to your score. A new tile appears after each move, so the board fills up while you chase 2048 and keep going past it. Every spawn comes from a seeded generator and every move is recorded, so a finished run is replayed server-side before it reaches the global leaderboard.
